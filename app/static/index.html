Custom rows for the Plex home screen

Define what belongs in a row, and Phlexerr keeps a Plex collection in sync and pins it to Home (like Maintainerr does). This only creates or edits collections; it never deletes media. It's separate from deletion candidates.

Several criteria = an item needs to match any one of them. Leave empty to use only the filters below.
List sources: IMDb / MDBList links or CSV upload
With lists, the row holds only the titles from them (combined) that are already in Plex. Use Order in the list as the sort to keep IMDb's order. Private lists: export the CSV from IMDb and upload it.

Getting started: what this app is

Phlexerr is pronounced “flex-er”.

Phlexerr looks at your Plex library together with Tautulli, Radarr, Sonarr and Seerr, and lists titles that are good candidates to delete: unwatched, old, low-rated, big, or downloaded without anyone requesting them. You stay in control: you review the list and decide what goes, and nothing is ever deleted on a schedule or by itself. It also protects media you want to keep, builds custom rows for the Plex Home Screen, and shows who is watching what.

  • Prune: review the list and delete. Protect: lock things from deletion. Plex Home Screen: custom rows and lists. History: everything that was deleted, and what it freed. Activity: who is watching right now, and watch history.
  • Data is read when you press Refresh data (and cached, so the page loads fast). Nothing changes in Plex until you delete or publish a row.
Safety: will it delete things by accident?
  • Dry run is the default. While DRY_RUN=true (simulation mode) nothing is deleted; you just see what would happen. It can only be switched in the container settings, not from the web page.
  • A real delete needs you to type DELETE in a dialog that lists every title and the space freed.
  • Protected and Keep items can't be selected, and the server refuses to delete them even if asked.
  • There is a batch limit (MAX_DELETE_BATCH, default 200). Every attempt, dry or live, is logged.
Prune: filtering, sorting and deleting
  • Filters: search text, type, library, requester, rating, year, size, watched state, "not watched in X days", "added within last X days", and Animation (include / hide / only). Reset clears them all.
  • Quick filters apply common combinations in one click: Unrequested downloads (recently added, nobody requested it in Seerr, nobody has watched it; adjust the two day values to taste), Never watched, Stale 2+ years, Low rated and Large files. Advanced filters opens the full set.
  • Switch between Cards and Table above the list (your choice is remembered). In the table, click a column header to sort; in cards, use the Sort dropdown. On a phone the filters fold behind a Filters button.
  • The ⓘ icon opens details with Open in Plex and IMDb buttons. A 🎨 badge marks animation.
  • Tick titles (or use select-all on the filtered list), then Delete. It removes the files via Radarr/Sonarr, clears the Seerr request so the title can be requested again, and writes to History.
  • Mark Keep protects the selected titles and hides them from the list (untick Hide protected items in Advanced filters to see them).
  • Export view as CSV saves whatever the list currently shows.
Leaving Soon: warn people before you delete (optional)
  • Off by default. If you just delete things, leave it off: there is no Leaving Soon row in Plex and no Leaving Soon buttons here. Turn it on in Settings → Leaving Soon.
  • When it is on, tick titles in Prune and press Leaving soon. They get a ⏳ badge with a due date and appear in a Leaving Soon row on your Plex Home, so people can watch them first. Not leaving takes them back out. Protected titles can't be marked.
  • Nothing is deleted automatically. When the date passes the badge turns to Past due and the title simply stays until you delete it. Use the Past due quick filter to find them, select them and delete in one go.
  • Turning the feature off removes the row from Plex Home. Your marks are kept, so turning it back on restores the row. Deleting a title (or it disappearing from Plex) clears its mark.
  • In Settings you can rename the row, set the default number of days, and choose whether it is pinned to Home.
Protect: rules and scorecard
  • Anything matching an enabled rule (or marked Keep) is locked. Rule types: actor, director, studio, collection, title, genre, tag, and Library (pick from a dropdown).
  • Matching ignores case and accents, and supports wildcards: * for any text, ? for one character. Example: Star Trek*.
  • Preview next to a rule lists exactly what it protects, with total disk size and per-title sizes. Disk size is also shown for every rule without clicking.
  • The scorecard shows protected disk space, how much of that was never watched, and the rules vs Keep split. Click column headers to sort rules.
  • Toggle a rule off to stop protecting without deleting it.
Plex Home Screen: custom rows
  • A row is a Plex collection that Phlexerr builds from your library and pins to the Home screen, then keeps up to date automatically.
  • Criteria: genre, tag/label, collection, actor, studio and so on, plus minimum IMDb rating, "added within X days", "unwatched only", and a limit.
  • Sorting: date added, rating, year, release date, play count, last watched, title, shuffle, list order, or My own order, each ascending or descending.
  • Shared Home: show the row on every user's Home (your Home users and shared users alike). Use ↑ / ↓ to set the order of all rows.
  • Publish runs in the background with a progress bar. The built-in Released on this day row is listed in the table too.
  • My own order opens an Arrange dialog: drag or move titles into your own sequence (handy for things like a Marvel timeline that isn't release order).
Lists from IMDb and MDBList
  • Paste one or more list links, or upload a CSV, and the row contains only the titles from those lists that you already have in Plex. Multiple lists are combined as a union.
  • IMDb sometimes blocks automated downloads (you may see HTTP 202). If so, export the list from IMDb and upload the CSV, or use a mdblist.com link instead. MDBList lists work without logging in; an optional MDBLIST_API_KEY helps with private lists.
  • Lists are cached, so repeated syncs are quick.
Missing titles: request what you don't have
  • On a list row, Missing… shows list titles that aren't in Plex. Each is checked against Seerr and marked Not requested, Already requested, Already available or Can't match.
  • Columns: poster thumbnail, release date, rating (IMDb/MDBList or Seerr), Seerr status. Default sort is release date, newest first; filter by text, status, type, minimum rating and Animation, and sort by any column.
  • Tick the ones you want and Request. Up to 50 per batch. A title is requested only when the title and year match exactly, so you don't get the wrong movie.
Request: search and request any title
  • Type at least two letters on the Request tab to search Seerr for movies and shows. The tab is hidden until Seerr is connected in Settings.
  • Each result shows the same status Seerr does: Available, Partially available, Requested (approved and on its way), Pending (waiting for approval) or Blocklisted. Titles that are already available, requested or pending have no Request button, so you can't duplicate a request.
  • Request sends it to Seerr as the API key's user, and Seerr passes it to Radarr or Sonarr as usual. Shows are requested with all seasons. Seerr may still need to approve it.
  • Nothing is requested until you press the button.
History, restore and re-request
  • History lists every deletion with size, requester, mode (dry/live) and result. Choose 25 / 50 / 100 / 250 / All per page (remembered).
  • Totals show space freed. Old entries get IMDb links filled in over time.
  • A deleted title can be re-requested through Seerr. If a title comes back later, the app detects it.
Activity: who is watching what

The Activity tab shows what is streaming right now in a Tautulli-style card (poster, product, player, quality, stream, container, video, audio, subtitle, location, bandwidth, end time; it updates by itself every 5 seconds and the progress bar moves in between, no refresh needed) and a searchable list of recent plays you can filter by user and type. It reads from Tautulli, so it needs the Tautulli URL and API key in Settings. The Stop button on a stream asks for confirmation and lets you send the viewer a message before ending playback. Everything else on this tab is read-only.

Settings, look and feel, phones
  • The ☾/☀ button switches light/dark (follows your OS until you choose).
  • Settings holds the service connections, the automatic refresh, the Released on this day row and the optional Leaving Soon row; Sign out ends your session (when a password is set).
  • On phones tables become cards. You can install the site as an app (Add to Home Screen); this needs HTTPS, e.g. a Cloudflare tunnel.
FAQ
"Never watched": whose watching counts?
Everyone's, from Tautulli history. Without Tautulli it falls back to the single Plex account.
Why is a title I expected missing from Prune?
It may be protected (a Protect rule or Keep) or hidden by a filter. Check the Protect tab and press Reset on the filters.
I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?
Files are removed, so no undelete, but History lets you re-request it through Seerr.
Why did a list sync fail?
The row shows the error. IMDb blocking is the usual cause; use a CSV upload or MDBList.
My row isn't on the Home screen.
Publish it, and make sure it's pinned. Users may need to refresh Plex; check "Shared Home" for other users.
Posters in the Missing list are blank.
The title hasn't been checked in Seerr yet or couldn't be matched. The server also needs outbound access to image.tmdb.org.
Which services do I need?
Only Plex. Tautulli, Radarr, Sonarr and Seerr are optional. Without Tautulli the play data comes from Plex itself and the Activity tab asks you to connect it. Without Radarr/Sonarr titles are deleted through Plex. Without Seerr the "requested by" details are hidden and the Missing list can't make requests. Add any of them later in Settings.
How do I update the app?
Drop phlexerr.zip into the appdata folder and run update.sh (or, with the ready-made image, use Unraid's Check for updates). Your settings and data in /config are kept. Hard-refresh the browser afterwards.
